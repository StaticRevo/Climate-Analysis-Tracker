## Weather Atlas | Climate Analysis Dashboard

**Weather Atlas** is an interactive web application designed to explore and visualise historical climate data across different cities worldwide. Powered by the Meteostat weather data service, the platform allows users to analyse long-term weather patterns, identify climate trends, and compare environmental conditions over selected periods.

### Key Features

- **Historical Climate Analysis:** Explore historical weather data for different cities using customisable date ranges.
- **Temperature Trends:** Visualise annual temperature changes and anomalies to identify long-term warming or cooling patterns.
- **Warming Signal Detection:** Compare average temperatures from the first and last five years of a selected period.
- **Climate Extremes:** Identify the warmest and wettest years within a selected timeframe.
- **Precipitation Analysis:** Examine annual rainfall totals and the frequency of wet days.
- **Wind Analysis:** Explore historical average wind speeds and variations over time.
- **Interactive Data Visualisation:** Interpret climate patterns through charts, statistics, and annual breakdowns.

### Project Objective

The project aims to make historical climate data more accessible and understandable through interactive visualisations. By transforming complex meteorological datasets into meaningful insights, Weather Atlas helps users explore environmental changes and better understand climate variability over time.
